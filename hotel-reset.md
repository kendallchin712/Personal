# The 60-Minute Hotel Reset

*Room → Villa · ~60 min · one pass at a time*

You already have the view, the pool, the styling. The only thing between your room and the holiday photo is about an hour of resetting — done in fast passes so you get quick wins instead of drowning. Put the Melbourne playlist on and work top to bottom.

**Rule: off the bed and floor first.**

---

## 1. Clothes (~20 min)
*80% of the visual noise. Two piles only — don't fold now, just clear.*
- [ ] Dirty clothes → straight into the laundry bag
- [ ] Clean clothes → one basket to deal with later
- [ ] Clear the bed completely — this alone transforms the room
- [ ] Clear the floor — nothing left down
- [ ] Bags & jackets onto hooks or into the wardrobe

## 2. Shopping bags (~10 min)
*The "mid-life-admin" signal that kills the calm.*
- [ ] Unpack every bag
- [ ] Decide keep or return — returns by the door
- [ ] Break down & recycle the paper/retail bags
- [ ] Put the keepers where they actually live

## 3. Surfaces (~10 min)
*Desk + nightstand. Leave only what's beautiful.*
- [ ] Water bottles, cups & plates → kitchen
- [ ] Rubbish binned
- [ ] Cables corralled / tucked
- [ ] Leave out only the candle, diffuser & one nice object

## 4. Bathroom (~10 min)
*A clear floor is 90% of a hotel bathroom.*
- [ ] Bags & boxes off the floor
- [ ] Hair tools into the basket / drawer
- [ ] Products lined up, extras stashed
- [ ] Quick wipe of the sink & mirror
- [ ] Fresh towel squared on the rail

## ✦ The turndown moment (~5 min)
*The payoff. Do this last and stand back.*
- [ ] Make the bed properly — pull it tight
- [ ] Open the blinds all the way to the view
- [ ] Light the candle · diffuser on
- [ ] Dim to lamps, not the harsh overhead
- [ ] Step back and actually look — that's your villa

---

## 🌙 The 10-minute nightly reset
*So it never gets back to square one — "never miss twice," applied to the room.*
- [ ] Clear every surface back to just the nice things
- [ ] Clothes away or in the basket — floor clear
- [ ] Cups & plates carried out
- [ ] Bed roughly straightened for the morning

---

*The one rule: uncover the villa, then keep it uncovered.*
