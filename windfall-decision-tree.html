Where the branches land

★ Recommended for you

12-month term deposit

BNZ · capital guaranteed

  • Can't lose it — the right instinct for inherited money.
  • Honours your mum's plan.
  • Locked away from impulse spending.
  • Doubles as a deep emergency backstop — breakable in a real crisis for a small penalty.
Why 12 months: at BNZ's rates the 1-year (~4.05%) turns $10k into ~$10,405 gross. Longer terms pay a little more, but the 1yr→2yr premium is tiny — not worth locking money you might need. Long enough to earn, short enough to reassess in a year.
Growth path

Invest it

Diversified fund via Sharesies

  • ~5–7%/yr expected long-run vs ~4% in a TD — real growth.
  • Compounds meaningfully over 5+ years.
  • You already use Sharesies.
Only if all three gates said "go": 5+ year horizon, you can watch a 20–30% drop without flinching, and you'll truly leave it. It means risking inherited money — real emotional weight.
You don't have to pick the endgame today — a 12-month TD is a valid "park & reassess." At maturity, with your emergency fund rebuilt, you can roll it, invest part, or aim it at a goal.
