# Resort Day at Home — Melbourne

*The at-home resort day · follow it loosely — the point is the pace, not the schedule*

You live in a building with a pool, a gym, and a skyline view — the exact brochure of a five-star hotel. A guest here would have the time of their life on an ordinary Saturday. This is their itinerary.

---

**8:30 — 🏊 Morning swim** · *Start in the water.* Down to the pool before the day gets loud. A slow swim or just a float. Your spa morning, an elevator ride away — no booking, no crowd.

**9:30 — ☕ Coffee with the view** · *The room-service moment.* Back up to a clear, made-up room, blinds open. Good coffee, something nice to eat, the skyline. No phone for the first bit — be the guest admiring the view they paid for.

**11:00 — 🗺️ Go explore** · *Play tourist in your own city.* Laneways, Queen Vic Market, a new café, the NGV, or a tram to St Kilda. If the weather's glorious, slot in a day trip instead — the coast is an hour away.

**3:00 — 🛋️ Poolside / rest, no guilt** · *The afternoon do-nothing.* A book by the pool, a lie-down, a slow shower like a spa. On holiday the afternoon is allowed to be empty. This is the part you always skip — don't.

**7:00 — 🍷 Dinner, done properly** · *Set the table like a guest.* Cook the special thing or bring home something lovely — but set the table, use the nice glass, light the candle, playlist on. Eat by the window as the city lights come up.

**9:00 — 🌆 Golden-hour wind-down** · *Close it like a holiday.* Lights low, view on, nowhere to be. Jot one photo or line about the day, the way you'd caption a trip. Tomorrow you wake up here again.

---

## 🎒 Night-before prep (5 min)
- Run the **hotel reset** so you wake up to a made room
- Swimmers & towel by the door
- Coffee/breakfast sorted so morning is effortless
- Phone on Do Not Disturb till after the swim

## ☔ Four-seasons-in-one-day backup
- Swap the explore for **indoor Melbourne** — NGV, ACMI, State Library dome, a laneway lunch
- Long **spa afternoon** in — bath, masks, the works
- A **rooftop bar**, or the view from your own window with a wine
- Cook a big, slow, holiday-style dinner and make it the event

---

*The trip isn't somewhere else. You already live in the postcard.*
