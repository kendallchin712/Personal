# Harada Method — Daily Energy Sprint

**Started:** 2026-08-03 · **Horizon:** 12 weeks (~2026-10-26)

---

## 🎯 Central Goal

> **For the next 12 weeks, build a daily-energy system so I wake up rested and feel genuinely energized 6 out of 7 days — by locking in my sleep, movement, food, and mind.**

The win isn't a number on a scale. It's the *habit itself* running on autopilot by week 12.

---

## The Center 3×3 (goal + 8 sub-goals)

| | | |
|---|---|---|
| 😴 **Sleep** | 🏃 **Movement** | 🥗 **Nutrition** |
| 📊 **Tracking** | 🎯 **DAILY ENERGY** | 💧 **Hydration** |
| 🧠 **Mind & Stress** | 🔄 **Daily Rhythm** | 🧹 **Environment** |

---

## 1. 😴 Sleep
- [ ] Set a fixed bedtime and wake time — same 7 days a week
- [ ] Aim for 7.5–8 hours in bed every night
- [ ] Bed is for sleep only — no work or scrolling in bed
- [ ] Keep the bedroom cool, dark, and quiet
- [ ] No caffeine after 2pm
- [ ] Get morning sunlight within 30 min of waking
- [ ] If awake >20 min at night, get up and reset (no scrolling)
- [ ] Track sleep nightly and note how rested I feel

## 2. 🏃 Movement
- [ ] Hit 8,000+ steps every day
- [ ] 3 real workouts a week (strength or cardio), 30+ min
- [ ] Take a 10-min walk after lunch
- [ ] Stand and stretch every hour during work
- [ ] 5-min mobility routine each morning
- [ ] Take the stairs by default
- [ ] Schedule workouts in my calendar like meetings
- [ ] Keep a backup 10-min home workout for busy days

## 3. 🥗 Nutrition
- [ ] Eat protein with every meal
- [ ] Eat a real breakfast — no skip-and-crash
- [ ] Add a vegetable or fruit to at least 2 meals a day
- [ ] Plan/prep meals so I'm not deciding while hungry
- [ ] Kill the afternoon sugar crash (protein/nuts instead)
- [ ] Stop eating 2–3 hrs before bed
- [ ] Keep junk out of the house; keep easy healthy options in
- [ ] Notice which foods drain vs. sustain my energy

## 4. 💧 Hydration & Intake
- [ ] Full glass of water right after waking
- [ ] Aim for ~2L of water a day (carry a bottle)
- [ ] Cap caffeine at 1–2 cups, all before 2pm
- [ ] Limit alcohol to weekends / special occasions
- [ ] Swap one soda or energy drink for water daily
- [ ] Hydrate before and after workouts
- [ ] Watch for the "tired but actually just thirsty" trap
- [ ] Track drinks (water, caffeine, alcohol) for awareness

## 5. 🧹 Environment
- [ ] Make the bed every morning
- [ ] 10-min tidy reset before bed — clear the surfaces
- [ ] Nothing left on the floor — clear it nightly
- [ ] Give everything a home; put it back right away
- [ ] Laundry before it overflows — never on the floor
- [ ] Carry out cups, plates & trash daily
- [ ] Friday 20-min deeper clean (vacuum, dust, reset)
- [ ] Declutter one small zone a week (drawer, shelf, corner)

## 6. 🔄 Daily Rhythm
*Morning bookend*
- [ ] No snooze — feet on the floor within 2 minutes
- [ ] No phone for the first 20–30 min
- [ ] Delay caffeine 60–90 min after waking
- [ ] 5-min plan: top 1–3 priorities for the day

*Evening bookend*
- [ ] Wind-down alarm ~1 hr before bed (same 7 days)
- [ ] Screens off & lights dimmed 30 min before bed
- [ ] A calming ritual (read, stretch, or shower)
- [ ] Brain-dump tomorrow's plan + worries onto paper

## 7. 🧠 Mind & Stress
- [ ] 5–10 min of breathing or meditation daily
- [ ] Get outside / into nature a few times a week
- [ ] Name my stressors weekly and what I can control
- [ ] Take real breaks — actually step away from the screen
- [ ] Protect one thing that recharges me (hobby, music, people)
- [ ] One line of gratitude daily
- [ ] Say no to one energy-draining thing this week
- [ ] Notice and interrupt the doom-scroll spiral

## 8. 📊 Tracking & Accountability
- [ ] Pick 3 keystone habits to track daily (sleep, steps, water)
- [ ] Log the day in under 60 seconds each night
- [ ] Weekly 10-min review: what worked, what didn't
- [ ] Tell one person my goal and check in with them
- [ ] Set phone reminders for key habits
- [ ] Celebrate small wins — don't wait for the finish line
- [ ] Aim for "80% consistent," not perfect — never miss twice
- [ ] Reassess and adjust the whole plan at week 6

---

*Rule of the sprint: never miss twice. Missing a day is human; missing two is the start of quitting.*
