🏠 Home-office running costs — your biggest claim

Not a receipt — a calculation. As a 5-day-a-week WFH worker, claim the ATO fixed rate (70c per hour worked from home for 2025-26), which covers electricity/gas (heating), internet, phone and stationery. Log your work-from-home hours. If Hnry uses this method, don't also itemise the Belong phone — it's already included. Equipment (chair, monitor arm) is separate and claimed on top either way.
