# Mood & Energy Log

A running log to spot patterns over time — is the "funk" cyclical (mood/mental health), situational, environmental, or hormonal? Each entry captures the observable signals so future-me can compare like with like instead of relying on memory.

**How to read this:** the point isn't to judge any single day. It's to line entries up later and look for rhythm — especially around sleep, cycle phase, and how "hermit mode" comes and goes.

---

## Entry template

```
### YYYY-MM-DD
- **Overall:** (a word or two — flat, wired, okay, heavy, light...)
- **Energy (0–10):**
- **Sleep:** (how it's been the last few nights; wake-up struggle?)
- **Cycle day / hormones:** (day of cycle if known, PMS-y?)
- **Left the house?:** (and why)
- **Self-care signals:** shower / laundry / cooking / hydration / nails / other
- **Social contact:** (who, how much, felt like effort?)
- **Work:** (able to focus? masking? camera-off days?)
- **What I meant to do vs. what I did:**
- **Anything notable / context:** (stress, events, weather, season)
- **Notes:**
```
