# Mood & Energy Log

A running log to spot patterns over time — is the "funk" cyclical (mood/mental health), situational, environmental, or hormonal? Each entry captures the observable signals so future-me can compare like with like instead of relying on memory.

**How to read this:** the point isn't to judge any single day. It's to line entries up later and look for rhythm — especially around sleep, cycle phase, and how "hermit mode" comes and goes.

#### What the flatness actually feels like (her words)
- **Not fatigue / not hypersomnia.** Energy feels roughly okay most days; not napping excessively (one nap today). So this isn't "physically depleted."
- **It's unstructured-time inertia.** Evenings and free time default to *passive* screen use — phone scrolling, window online-shopping (browsing, not buying), a series on in the background — instead of the *active / valued* things she actually enjoys (hobbies, reading, doing her nails). Ends the evening having "not done an activity of any shape," which then feeds the flat feeling.
- **Reading:** this is the anhedonia / low-*activation* flavour (the *wanting* and *initiating* is offline), overlaid on a luteal dip — rather than a body that's out of fuel. Passive leisure is the lowest-effort default, so a low-drive brain grabs it; but passive leisure doesn't refill the tank the way absorbing/active leisure does, so the flatness persists. Target for next time = gentle *behavioural activation*, not "try harder."
