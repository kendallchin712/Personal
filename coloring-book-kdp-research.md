# Colouring Book Niche Research — Amazon KDP Australia

*Research date: 23 August 2026 · Market: amazon.com.au · Prepared for a first-time publisher deciding whether to try this.*

---

## The one-line verdict

**As a "passive income" bet on a single book: no.** As a **~2-hour, ~A$0 experiment to learn the whole KDP pipeline in a niche that's actually chosen with data: yes — worth it, with realistic expectations.** The money in this space comes from *catalogues* (dozens of books) plus ads, not one lucky listing. But there are still genuine low-competition gaps, and Australia is a legitimately good marketplace to target.

---

## Why Australia is actually a decent choice

- **AU now prints locally.** Amazon opened a print-on-demand facility in Western Sydney in 2021, so paperbacks sold on amazon.com.au print domestically — faster delivery, better buy-box, no "ships from overseas" penalty. My initial worry about AU being structurally weak was outdated.
- **Less saturated than the US.** The US market is where every "AI passive income" course points its audience. amazon.com.au has a fraction of the competing titles, and **Australian-relevant themes give you a keyword + relevance edge that US publishers don't bother chasing.**
- **Royalty math (AU, 2026):** 60% royalty if you list at **AUD 13.99 or above**, 50% below that (the threshold dropped in June 2025). Colouring books have a **black-ink interior** (the buyer supplies the colour), so they print at cheap B&W rates (~A$2.50–3.50 for a 40–60pp book). List at **A$13.99–16.99** → roughly **A$5–8 royalty per sale**. Healthy margin.

---

## How to read "competition"

The broad term "colouring book" has **100,000+ titles** — unwinnable. But **micro-niches routinely run under 500 direct competitors** and, positioned right, earn far more per title. The whole game in 2026 is: **niche down until the keyword is beatable, then stack two themes no one has paired.** A "bold-and-easy quokka colouring book for adults" beats a "beautiful animal colouring book" every time.

> ⚠️ **Data honesty:** This environment can't reach amazon.com.au directly (it's blocked), so the numbers below are **triangulated from multiple web sources, not live BSR/Keepa pulls.** They're directionally reliable for a go/no-go decision, but before you commit hours to a specific title, spend 20 minutes validating it live (see "Verify before you build").

## The 2026 market in three facts

1. **"Bold & Easy" / "Cozy" is the dominant style** — thick simple outlines, one large design per page. It replaced the intricate "Secret Garden" era. Appeals to seniors, beginners, and stress-relief buyers. Commands US$8.99–12.99. **But the top of this category is locked up by brands like Coco Wyo (~40–50% of the top 50 adult titles)** — so you win on *specificity*, not by publishing "Bold & Easy Animals."
2. **Adult colouring is a growing market** — ~US$151M (2024) heading toward US$320M+ by 2030, driven by the wellness / screen-free movement.
3. **Q4 is everything for gifts** — ~40% of purchases are gifts; seasonal/Christmas titles must be live by **August–September** to rank in time.

## Top 3 recommendations

### 🥇 1. Bold & Easy — Australian cute animals
**Why it's the pick:** it stacks four advantages at once — the *dominant style* (bold & easy), a *proven-cute subject*, the *AU marketplace relevance* edge, and the format AI is genuinely best at (thick, simple, forgiving line art).

- **Angle / title idea:** *"Bold & Easy Aussie Animals: Big, Simple Colouring for Adults & Kids — Quokkas, Wombats, Koalas & Cuddly Native Friends"*
- **Sub-angles to pick ONE and own:** baby native animals · "grumpy/cheeky" Aussie animals · quokkas only (internet-famous, low competition as a solo subject)
- **Specs:** 8.5×11" · 40–55 single-sided pages · one bold design per page, blank backs to prevent bleed-through
- **Price:** A$14.99 (keeps you in the 60% royalty band)
- **Main risk:** "bold & easy animals" broadly is crowded — your defensibility is the *specific* Aussie subject + a consistent, recognisable cover style. Don't publish generic "cute animals."

### 🥈 2. Large-print bold colouring for AU seniors
**Why:** the 65+ population keeps growing and is genuinely underserved; buyers (and gift-givers / aged-care staff) are highly motivated, not casual browsers. AI excels at the required thick, simple, one-per-page art. Pairs beautifully with an Australian twist for differentiation.

- **Angle / title idea:** *"Easy Large-Print Colouring Book for Seniors: Familiar Australian Scenes — Simple, Bold Designs for Relaxation, Low Vision & Dementia Care"*
- **Why the AU twist matters:** "familiar scenes" (a Hills Hoist, a beach shack, native birds, a country verandah) provide cognitive familiarity for dementia care — a real, marketable benefit competitors' generic books lack.
- **Specs:** 8.5×11" · 30–50 pages · very thick outlines, high contrast, minimal detail
- **Price:** A$13.99–15.99
- **Main risk:** you must genuinely nail *thick lines + big shapes* — reviewers in this niche punish anything fiddly. Test-print one page and check it at arm's length.

### 🥉 3. A single trend animal (ride the wave)
**Why:** lowest-friction way to learn the pipeline. Axolotls, capybaras, highland cows, quokkas are "characterful sub-niches" with big demand and medium competition. Almost zero AU-specificity, so you compete globally — but it's the fastest, most AI-friendly first build.

- **Title idea:** *"Bold & Easy Capybara Colouring Book: Chill, Cute & Simple Designs for Adults and Kids"*
- **Specs / price:** as above, A$13.99–14.99
- **Main risk:** trend timing — these can cool off; don't over-invest a series here.

## Niches to be careful with (or avoid)

- **❌ Licensed / branded anything** — Bluey, Pokémon, Frozen, Sanrio, sports teams. Huge AU temptation, **guaranteed IP takedown + possible account termination.** Never.
- **⚠️ Hyper-local landmarks (Sydney Opera House, MCG, your home town)** — genuinely under-served and defensible (only a handful of indie titles like *Local Lines – Sydney* and *Mixed Business – Melbourne* exist), **but AI hallucinates recognisable architecture badly.** Doable only if you trace/heavily edit real reference photos — that's a *manual-illustration* project, not a 2-hour AI build. Great future idea, wrong first project.
- **⚠️ Text-heavy niches (affirmations, snarky gag gifts, wellness journals)** — AI still can't render clean, correctly-spelled typography. You'd hand-set every word. Fine if you're comfortable in Canva/Affinity, painful otherwise.
- **⚠️ Dog-breed accuracy** — AI often gets proportions/breed markings wrong; needs careful curation. Pick a breed you know well enough to reject bad outputs.
- **❌ Generic "adult colouring book," mandalas, "animals," kids dot-marker at scale** — saturated to the point a new listing is invisible.

## If you proceed — the build checklist

1. **Validate the exact title live first** (see below) — 20 min, non-negotiable.
2. **Generate interior line art** — bold & easy prompts, black line on white, no grey fills, no shading. Generate extra; keep only genuinely clean ones.
3. **Clean each page** — remove stray marks, ensure closed lines, convert to true black/white, **300 DPI**.
4. **Lay out the PDF** — 8.5×11", one design per page, **single-sided (blank backs)**, correct **inside margins/gutter** and **bleed** per KDP's template for your page count.
5. **Cover** — KDP Cover Creator or a Canva front + spine + back; show 2–3 sample pages on the back.
6. **KDP metadata:**
   - **Title + subtitle** carrying your main keywords ("Bold & Easy", "Australian", "Seniors", the animal).
   - **7 backend keywords** — long-tail phrases a buyer would type, not single words.
   - **2 browse categories** — the specific colouring sub-categories, not just "Books".
7. **✅ Tick the AI-content disclosure box.** KDP *requires* you to disclose AI-generated images (even if you edited them). It's **private** (no public badge, no ranking penalty), but **undisclosed AI content risks title suppression or account suspension.** Always disclose.
8. **Price** at **A$13.99+** for the 60% band; order **one author proof copy** before going live and actually colour a page to check paper/bleed.

## Verify before you build (do this, it's the step the videos skip)

Because I couldn't pull live Amazon data, spend ~20 minutes confirming your specific idea *on amazon.com.au itself*:

- Search your exact title phrase (e.g. "bold and easy aussie animals"). **Few, weak, low-review results = a gap. A wall of polished titles = pick a tighter angle.**
- Check the **Best Sellers Rank** of the top few results (lower number = selling more). If nothing in the niche ranks under ~200,000 in Books, demand may be too thin.
- Note their **price, page count, and cover quality** — that's the bar you must clear.
- *(Optional upgrade)* Publisher Rocket (~US$97, one-off) or the free **DS Amazon Quick View** browser extension turn this guesswork into real numbers. Worth it only once you're doing this repeatedly.

---

## Realistic expectations

- A **single** first book, no ads, no marketing: expect **a handful to a few dozen sales** over months. That's normal — it's tuition, not income.
- "Passive income" here = a **system**: a repeatable niche→generate→format→publish workflow, run across **many** titles, ideally with a small Amazon Ads budget and Q4 seasonal timing.
- **The realistic win from your 2 hours** isn't the royalties — it's owning the end-to-end pipeline so book #2 takes 45 minutes, and knowing (from data) which niche is worth #2 through #10.
