# 30-Day Pinterest → Benable Starter Plan

*Goal: turn your existing Benable lists from a $12/year trickle into a real (if light) traffic channel — using Pinterest, your two proven niches (fantasy romance + skincare), and a sustainable weekly habit. Built 23 Aug 2026.*

---

## The strategy in one paragraph

Pinterest is an evergreen **search engine for shoppers** (631M users; a pin drives traffic for 6–12+ months, vs 24–48 hrs on Instagram). You already know two things that convert for you: **fantasy-romance books** (huge BookTok/Pinterest demand, easy clicks, *low* commission) and **skincare** (high demand, *high* 10–30% commission). So the play is: **use book pins to pull people in, skincare pins to make the money**, all pointing at your Benable lists. This is a light content habit, not passive — but it's the lowest-effort real growth move you have, because the lists already exist.

---

## Your one success metric (read this before anything)

**Do NOT judge this by sales in 30 days.** Pinterest is a slow burn and commissions lag (purchase + Benable attribution). Judge by **leading indicators** in Pinterest Analytics:

- ✅ **Keep going** if by Day 30 you have a clear upward trend: **1,000+ monthly impressions and 40+ outbound clicks**, growing week over week.
- ⚠️ **Diagnose** (fix keywords/pin design) if impressions are near-zero after consistent pinning — that means your pins aren't matching searches.
- 💰 Real commissions typically start trickling in **month 2–3**, not month 1. That's normal.

Set a calendar reminder for Day 30 to check these numbers and make the call.

---

## Phase 1 — Setup (Days 1–3, ~2 hours total)

1. **Create a free Pinterest *Business* account** (not personal — you need Analytics). Use a niche-flavoured name, e.g. *"[YourName] Reads & Routines"* or split later if you want two accounts.
2. **Complete the profile**: keyword-rich bio ("Fantasy romance book recs + skincare routines for glowing skin"), profile photo, and **claim your website** if you have one (optional).
3. **Tidy your Benable lists** first — make sure your book + skincare lists are complete (12–15 items each), with keyword-rich notes on each item. Chase Benable's **Optimized List Badge** (it gets you extra Benable-internal traffic for free).
4. **Confirm auto-affiliate links are ON** in Benable so every list link is monetised.
5. **Create 6 boards** (keyword-named — the board name *is* SEO):

   **Book side (traffic engine):**
   - `Fantasy Romance Book Recommendations`
   - `Books to Read After ACOTAR / Romantasy TBR`
   - `Cozy Reading Nook & Bookish Gifts`

   **Skincare side (money engine):**
   - `Korean Skincare Routine for Glowing Skin`
   - `Holy Grail Skincare Products`
   - `Skincare Gift Guide & Under-$50 Finds`

   Give each board a 1–2 sentence keyword-rich description.

---

## Phase 2 — First pins (Days 4–7, ~2–3 hours)

**Pin design recipe (make a reusable Canva template):**
- Size **1000 × 1500 px** (2:3 ratio — the Pinterest standard).
- Bold, readable text overlay (the "hook") on an appealing image.
- Consistent fonts/colours so your pins look like a brand.
- **Each pin needs: a keyword-rich title + a 2–3 sentence keyword-rich description + the destination link (your Benable list).**

**Make 3–5 *different* pin designs per list** — Pinterest rewards "fresh" images, and multiple designs let you test which hook wins, all pointing to the same list.

**Pin title examples that match how people actually search:**

*Books:*
- "15 Fantasy Romance Books to Read if You Loved ACOTAR"
- "The Ultimate Romantasy Reading List for 2026"
- "Sarah J. Maas Books in Order (Where to Start)"
- "Spicy Fantasy Books for Your Fall TBR"

*Skincare:*
- "My 8-Step Korean Skincare Routine for Glowing Skin"
- "Best Hydrating Skincare Products Under $50"
- "The Skincare That Actually Cleared My Skin (Holy Grails)"
- "Dewy Skin Starter Kit — Beginner Skincare Routine"

**Compliance (don't skip):** add a brief disclosure to pin descriptions or your bio — e.g. *"Contains affiliate links."* It's an FTC/Pinterest requirement and it's one line.

---

## Phase 3 — Build the habit (Days 8–30)

Consistency beats bursts. Pinterest favours steady, fresh pinning.

**Weekly cadence (~30–45 min/day, 5 days/week):**

| Days | Focus | Target |
|---|---|---|
| **Week 1** (1–7) | Setup + first pins | Boards live + ~10 pins published |
| **Week 2** (8–14) | Cadence + keyword research | ~3–5 fresh pins/day; note which topics get impressions |
| **Week 3** (15–21) | Double down | Make more designs of your best-performing pins; push **skincare** (money) pins harder |
| **Week 4** (22–30) | Optimise + decide | Keep pinning; Day 30 review against the success metric |

**Keyword research (10 min, repeatable):** type a seed word into the Pinterest search bar and use the **autocomplete suggestions** + the coloured **guided-search tiles** — those are literally what people search. Put those exact phrases into your pin titles/descriptions.

**Daily loop:** pin 3–5 fresh designs → spend 5 min saving a couple of *other* people's relevant pins to your boards (signals activity) → done.

## The money asymmetry (keep this front of mind)

- **Books = traffic & warm audience**, but Amazon book commissions are tiny (your $0.71–$1.95 transactions). Don't expect income from these — they're the top of the funnel.
- **Skincare = the actual money** (10–30% commissions, $50–200 items). As soon as your book pins start pulling views, **weight your effort toward skincare pins.** That's where clicks turn into real dollars.

---

## Realistic outcomes

- **Casual (a few pins/week):** reliably cross Benable's **$80 payout floor**, maybe **$20–50/mo within 6–12 months**. Real, compounding pocket money.
- **Committed (daily-ish, keyworded, consistent):** a **few hundred $/month within ~12 months** is plausible given your head start — treat the widely-quoted "$100–500/mo in 3–6 months" beginner range as the optimistic end.

---

## The bigger payoff

The Pinterest traffic skill is worth **more than Benable's cut.** Once pins are working, the same audience and boards can later point at *higher-margin* things: direct skincare-brand affiliate programs (10–30%), your own digital product, or even your own KDP book in the fantasy-romance niche. **Benable is the frictionless way to monetise while you learn the traffic skill — and the skill is the real asset.**

---

## Day-30 decision gate

Open Pinterest Analytics and check the trend:
- **Impressions climbing + 40+ outbound clicks + any Benable commission movement** → it's working; keep the habit and lean into skincare.
- **Flat/near-zero impressions** → it's a *discoverability* problem, not a demand problem. Fix pin titles/descriptions to match real search terms (redo the keyword step) and give it another 30 days before judging.
- **Impressions good but zero clicks** → your pin hooks/designs aren't compelling; test new designs.

Don't quietly abandon it at a trickle again — either commit to the light habit, or consciously park it. The worst outcome is half-effort.
