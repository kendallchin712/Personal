Everyday Holiday · The at-home resort day

A resort day, no airport required

You live in a building with a pool, a gym, and a skyline view — the exact brochure of a five-star hotel. A guest here would have the time of their life on an ordinary Saturday. This is their itinerary. Follow it loosely; the point is the pace, not the schedule.

🏊
8:30 — MORNING SWIM

Start in the water

Down to the pool before the day gets loud. A slow swim or just a float. This is your spa morning, and it's an elevator ride away — no booking, no crowd.

☕
9:30 — COFFEE WITH THE VIEW

The room-service moment

Back up to a clear, made-up room, blinds open. Good coffee, something nice to eat, and that skyline. No phone for the first bit — just be the guest admiring the view they paid for.

🗺️
11:00 — GO EXPLORE

Play tourist in your own city

Out into Melbourne with fresh eyes: the laneways, Queen Vic Market, a new café, the NGV, or a tram to St Kilda. If the weather's glorious, this is where a day-trip slots in instead — the coast is an hour away.

🛋️
3:00 — POOLSIDE / REST, NO GUILT

The afternoon do-nothing

A book by the pool, a lie-down, a slow shower like a spa. On holiday the afternoon is allowed to be empty. Let it be. This is the part you always skip — don't.

🍷
7:00 — DINNER, DONE PROPERLY

Set the table like a guest

Cook the special thing or bring home something lovely — but set the table, use the nice glass, light the candle, put the playlist on. Eat by the window as the city lights come up.

🌆
9:00 — GOLDEN HOUR WIND-DOWN

Close it like a holiday

Lights low, view on, nowhere to be. Maybe jot one photo or line about the day — the way you'd caption a trip. Tomorrow you wake up here again.

🎒 Night-before prep (5 min)

  • Run the hotel reset so you wake up to a made room
  • Swimmers & towel by the door
  • Coffee/breakfast sorted so morning is effortless
  • Phone on Do Not Disturb till after the swim

☔ Four-seasons-in-one-day backup

  • Swap the explore for indoor Melbourne — NGV, ACMI, State Library dome, a laneway lunch
  • Long spa afternoon in — bath, masks, the works
  • A rooftop bar or the view from your own window with a wine
  • Cook a big, slow, holiday-style dinner and make it the event
The trip isn't somewhere else. You already live in the postcard.
